## The challenge

People looking for everyday care often had to compare providers, availability, and costs across disconnected tools.

## The approach

We spoke with patients and care teams, mapped the search-to-visit journey, and designed a mobile-first service that makes the next step easy to understand.

## The outcome

Patients could find care with more confidence, while the team gained a shared service blueprint to guide future improvements.
