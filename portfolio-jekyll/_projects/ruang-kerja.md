## The challenge

Ruang Kerja brought tasks, conversations, and team rituals together, but its growing feature set made the product harder to navigate for new teams.

## The approach

We simplified the information architecture around how teams plan their week, then built a flexible component system to keep collaboration patterns consistent across the product.

## The outcome

The new workspace gave teams a more legible home for their work and gave the product team a stronger foundation for shipping new features.
