## The challenge

Karsa wanted to help first-time investors build a healthier relationship with money. The existing experience had too many decisions up front, leaving new customers unsure where to begin.

## The approach

I partnered with the product team to map the first-time journey, identify moments of uncertainty, and prototype a calmer onboarding flow. We brought account setup, goal setting, and a first deposit into one guided experience.

## The outcome

The redesigned flow helped the team improve activation while giving customers a clearer understanding of what happens next. The visual system also became the foundation for the wider product.
